# Additional sculk media

The sensor, calibrated sensor and shrieker meshes are original to this project and licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The new `mcl_sculk_click`, `mcl_sculk_shriek` and `mcl_sculk_warning` sound effects are original synthesized audio and available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The sculk vein, calibrated sensor PNGs, sensor tendrils, shrieker inner surfaces and vibration particle come from [Pixel Perfection Legacy 26.3-97.2](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/pixel-perfection-legacy/version/26.3-97.2) by NovaWostra and contributors. The pack continues [Pixel Perfection by Hugh “XSSheep” Rutland](https://www.planetminecraft.com/texture-pack/131pixel-perfection/). The vein repeats its first frame to match the four-frame atlas.
